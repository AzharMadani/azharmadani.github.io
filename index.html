⚖️Dispute Court
🤖 Dispute Court: Multi-Agent AI for Card Disputes
A decision-support system that runs credit card disputes and chargebacks as a mock trial. Customer and merchant advocate agents argue from verified records, a verifier drops uncited claims, and a judge applies network and Reg E/Z rules. A calibrated LightGBM model then turns the ruling into a win probability, and borderline or high-value cases go to a human reviewer.
